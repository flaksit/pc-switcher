## Keys chosen

`&lt;y&gt;` apply · `&lt;s&gt;` skip once · `&lt;n&gt;` always skip · `&lt;space&gt;` cycles the focused row · shift of any key sets **every** row · `&lt;enter&gt;` confirm · `&lt;ctrl-c&gt;` abort.

`&lt;y&gt;` because the act word varies per group (`install`, `remove`, `overwrite`, `delete repository`) so no first letter is stable; `&lt;s&gt;`/`&lt;n&gt;` are mnemonic and distinct. `&lt;a&gt;` is not merely avoided — `decision_list.FORBIDDEN_KEY` rejects it at construction (`decision_list.py:59,289`), so no future caller has to remember. Shift-of-key replaces questionary's mislabelled `&lt;a&gt; to toggle` and the legend says what it does. `&lt;space&gt;` exists for a user who did not read the legend.
